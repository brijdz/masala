What it does: pads or scales to 1080×1920 on the brand background, lays the accent rule and MAIDAN & MASALA wordmark over every frame, keeps the mark clear of Facebook's bottom UI strip, and re-encodes to H.264.

Speed: roughly one to three minutes for a 60-second clip. It is real video encoding, in a browser tab — leave the tab in the foreground while it runs.

It does not add music, narration or captions. Music goes on in Facebook's own composer, where it is licensed for Reels.
